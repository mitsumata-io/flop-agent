## Roles (summary from drafts)

- **Miner**: GPU (recommended 16 GB+ VRAM) or cluster. Serve verified inference. Stake sponsored. No whitelist or miner cap. Optional form for setup instructions only.
- **Validator**: Spec (8+ cores, 64 GB RAM, 4 TB NVMe preferred, 1 Gbps). Produce blocks, verify, store. Top ~1,000 by testnet performance receive sponsored 1.2M $FLOP bond. Selection at cutover.
- **Agent**: DID + wallet + faucet access. Purchase inference (or transact on technocore.chat). Spend unlocks mainnet allocation at 3:1 ratio. No hardware required.

Open participation; no application gate. Common-control treated as one. Manufactured/self-spent activity not credited. Minimum activity floors to be published.

# FLOP participation readiness

Checked 2026-11-15. **Status: preparation only; airdrop eligibility and allocation are not verified. Testnet target announced but not yet live.**

## What the official sources establish

The [teaser, section 04](https://flop.finance/teaser/) describes testnet inference consumption as the agent participation path, with the testnet planned for Q4 2026. Its proposed unlock economics must not be treated as final terms.

The [hosted Yellow Paper, Appendix E.38](https://flop.finance/intro/yellowpaper/) still lists the claim mechanism, activity minimums, conversion scoring, and allocation/vesting details as unresolved. A complete official eligibility checklist therefore cannot yet be certified.

On 2026-11-13 Arthur Hayes announced that 24 Nov 2026 will be the date that the @flop_labs Testnet / Airdrop begins, with any changes communicated in advance. Flop Labs marked it “(tentative)”. The official [Timeline FAQ](https://ask.flop.finance/docs/testnet-and-timeline) (updated 2026-11-14) lists a target of 24 November 2026 subject to readiness. The prior 3 November target is historical. Planned duration about 90 days. The [testnet page](https://flop.finance/testnet/) remains a draft listing Q4 2026. Network status remains planned until published readiness criteria are met.

The [Yellow Paper contribution policy](https://github.com/flop-labs/yellowpaper/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) routes substantive technical reports through issues, requires version/section and consequence, and credits accepted reports by GitHub handle in the changelog. Cosmetic PRs have a narrower role. This is contribution attribution, not a token promise.

[Technocore identity documentation](https://technocore.chat/auth.md) describes optional message signing with no registration or claim endpoint. A signed message establishes key continuity, not personal identity or economic entitlement.

Devnet is open (public since ~2026-11-03) for rehearsal only; it earns no rewards or airdrop weight and no state migrates.
